# Lombok Devlab design direction

## Identity

Lombok Devlab is a web development studio based in Lombok. The interface should feel clear, capable, and locally grounded, with the angular LD mark as its visual signature.

## Palette

- Navy `#081429`: primary text, navigation, and structural emphasis.
- Red `#CB222A`: the brand mark and a restrained focal accent.
- Near-white `#F8FBFB`: the main page surface.
- Slate `#C7CFDA`: supporting surfaces, separators, and quiet structure.

Use tints of these colors for state and readability, not unrelated accent hues. Keep red to key emphasis rather than repeating it on every label or icon. Portfolio previews may retain distinct project colors because they depict example client interfaces, not the Lombok Devlab interface.

## Type and form

Use Space Grotesk for headings because its angular geometry relates to the LD mark. Use DM Sans for body copy and controls for clear reading. Keep cards mostly square-cornered, with borders and spacing doing more work than shadows or decorative badges.

## Composition and motion

Use direct, editorial layouts with clear hierarchy and varied section compositions. Repeated cards are appropriate for comparable team members, but should stay quiet so names and roles lead. Motion is limited to brief hover and focus feedback. Avoid ambient animation, generic gradients, and decorative status dots.

Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.
