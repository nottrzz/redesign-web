Frequently asked questions about web development.

Useful answers for planning a business website.

How long does website development take?

A landing page typically takes 3–5 business days, a company website 7–14 business days, and an online store around 3–5 weeks. Timelines begin when scope and content are ready; custom systems depend on their specifications.

Are domain, hosting, and SEO included?

Packages include a .my.id domain for the first year, SSL, responsive design, basic SEO, and two review rounds. Hosting and premium domains are discussed separately. SEO covers technical foundations and metadata.

Can you build an online store with payment integration?

Yes. We can scope a checkout and payment provider around your products and order flow. Provider setup, transaction fees, and any third-party subscriptions are confirmed as part of the project.

Can an existing website be redesigned or expanded?

Yes. Share the current site, what is not working, and the changes you need. We can discuss a redesign, maintenance, or new features based on the existing setup.

What should I prepare before a consultation?

Bring a short overview of your business, who the website is for, its main goal, and any features you already have in mind. You do not need a technical specification to get started.
