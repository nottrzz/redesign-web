Three useful questions

Plan a website without getting stuck on technical terms.

These straightforward answers can help you prepare for an initial discussion with a web developer.

01 · Website type

Landing page or company website?

A landing page fits one offer and one primary action. A company profile works when people need to understand several services, your organisation, and how you work. Compare services.

02 · Website costs

What affects a project estimate?

Price depends on pages, features, content, design, integrations, and the workflow to build. Lombok Devlab starting prices: IDR 500,000 for a landing page, IDR 1.5 million for a company profile, IDR 5 million for e-commerce, and IDR 6.9 million for custom websites.

03 · Project preparation

What materials should you gather?

Start with your audience, products or services, page goals, contact details, logo, and images you are authorised to use. Confirm the final content list after agreeing on project scope.

04 · Functionality

When should you consider custom web development?

Discuss a custom build when you need user roles, dashboards, approval flows, integrations, specialised bookings, or data processes that standard information pages cannot support.

Search visibility

SEO-friendly is not a promise of page-one rankings.

Technical foundations and well-structured content help search engines understand a website. Visibility also depends on keyword competition, how well a page answers a search, site reputation, technical health, and ongoing improvements.

Start with one page for one search need, publish genuinely useful information, connect related pages, then measure and refine. No provider can guarantee the number-one position for every keyword.
