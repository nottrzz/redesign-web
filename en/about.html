How projects start

Start with the problem, not technical jargon.

You do not need a complete specification to start. Explain your goals and workflow; we can identify the technical requirements together.

  1. 01
    Understand the goal

    Identify the users, information, and tasks the website needs to support.

  2. 02
    Set the scope

    Agree on pages, content, features, integrations, and project boundaries.

  3. 03
    Build and review

    Develop the website in stages so the content and functions can be reviewed.

  4. 04
    Plan what comes next

    Maintenance and further development can be discussed separately.

Technology and services

Projects can include UI/UX design, company websites, landing pages, e-commerce, web applications, and information systems. Depending on the brief, options may include Laravel, React, Next.js, Tailwind CSS, custom WordPress, REST APIs, and payment gateway integrations.
